# Performance-mode QA

Semantic text, performance text and delivery target are separate contracts.
Neutral/fast speech keeps content and final-word gates. Whisper, shout, crying,
scream-speech and laughter relax energy/pitch diagnostics while retaining
timing/clipping safeguards. Efforts may be non-linguistic and therefore do not
get a fabricated lexical/final-word verdict. An explicit manifest mode always
beats heuristic classification; heuristic evidence is diagnostic.
